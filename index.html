The Circle of Control

Change gets harder when you spend your energy on what you can't control. Sort it into three circles.

  • What I controlMy actions, effort, boundaries, habits, decisions and response.
  • What I can influenceRelationships, opportunities, conversations, reputation and some outcomes.
  • What I cannot controlOther people's decisions and everything that has already happened.

The Action Ladder

When you're struggling to begin, climb one rung at a time.

  • 1. ThinkClarify what you actually want to do.
  • 2. PrepareIdentify what you need.
  • 3. StartTake the smallest practical action.
  • 4. RepeatDo another small action tomorrow.

The Mistake Recovery Framework

When something goes wrong, four steps turn the mistake into information.

  • 1. AcknowledgeName what happened accurately. Don't exaggerate it or minimize it.
  • 2. UnderstandAsk why: planning, information, emotion, communication or a missing skill.
  • 3. RepairFix what can be fixed and accept what can't be changed.
  • 4. AdjustDecide what you'll do differently next time.

The Life Audit

Before changing your life, understand your current one. Take a blank page and divide it into eight areas. Press the ones that need attention.

    0 of 8 need attention

    For each area, ask what is working, what is not, what needs attention and what can wait. The goal is awareness, not perfection.
